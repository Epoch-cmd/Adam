ShinyHunt

Eight gyms. One champion. Your knowledge is the attack.

Every turn you get a prompt and 15 seconds. Any correct answer hits, but the rarer it is, the harder it hits. A Shiny answer is an instant KO. Run out of time and the leader hits back.

Unofficial fan-made trivia, not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company. Gym leaders are original characters. Rarity tiers are hand-picked estimates.
