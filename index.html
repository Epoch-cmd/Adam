ShinyHunt

Choose your partner. Beat eight gyms. Rare answers hit harder.

Every turn you get a prompt and 15 seconds. Wrong guesses? Keep typing. The rarer your answer, the stronger the move, and a Shiny answer is an instant KO. Your partner evolves after badges 2 and 5. Opponents are random every run.
